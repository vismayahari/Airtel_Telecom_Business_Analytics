# 📊 Airtel Telecom Business Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Airtel Telecom Business Analytics Dashboard** is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The project focuses on analyzing telecom business data and transforming it into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards and visualizations.

The dashboard helps understand business performance, customer behavior, revenue trends, and plan performance to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall telecom business performance.
- Monitor revenue and customer-related KPIs.
- Understand customer distribution and churn patterns.
- Evaluate recharge and plan performance.
- Identify state-wise business trends.
- Present key insights through interactive visualizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **DAX** – Measures and calculated metrics.
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data preparation and analysis.
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships between fact and dimension tables.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview
- Business performance overview.
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
- Monthly revenue trends.
- Customer distribution analysis.
- State-wise business analysis.

### 2. Customer Analytics
- Customer segmentation.
- Customer behavior analysis.
- Churn analysis.
- Customer-related performance insights.

### 3. Plan Analysis
- Recharge and plan performance.
- Plan-wise revenue analysis.
- Comparison of different plans.
- Insights into plan usage and performance.

## ✨ Key Features

- Interactive slicers and filters.
- KPI cards for business performance monitoring.
- Monthly revenue trend analysis.
- Customer and plan analysis.
- State-wise visualizations.
- Dynamic DAX calculations.
